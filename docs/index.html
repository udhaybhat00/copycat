A FEW THINGS, CLEARLY.

Good questions.
Straight answers.

When does the free trial start?＋

On your first Copycat session, after setup. The trial lasts 10 minutes from that point and is tied to your device. Closing or reinstalling the app does not restart it. An internet connection is required to establish the trial.

Is AI usage included in the monthly price?＋

The subscription covers Copycat software access. Bring your own API key for answer generation. Your chosen provider bills its usage separately. Local model options depend on your configuration and hardware.

How does activation and renewal work?＋

The app opens a prefilled email with your Device ID. After confirming payment, Udhay issues your device-bound key. Paste it once. Renewals extend the same key while the subscription remains valid.

Does everything stay on my device?＋

Local transcription and local storage are available. Cloud AI and speech services receive the data required for their features when you select them. License checks send a device identifier and entitlement information, not meeting audio or transcript content.

Will answers always appear instantly?＋

Answers stream as they are generated. Timing and accuracy depend on audio quality, the speech model, the chosen AI provider, network conditions, and your device. Review suggestions before using them.
