Setup, downloads & support

Get the right installer, troubleshoot a session, or contact Udhay about device activation and renewals.

Choose your installer

Apple Silicon Macs use the arm64 build. Intel Macs use the Intel build. Windows downloads are x64. Get official files from the Downloads section or the linked GitHub release. Version 1.1.0 Mac builds are not Developer ID notarized; Windows installers are unsigned. Check the release notes before installing.

Start with a short test

Configure a speech model and answer provider in Settings. Play meeting audio, watch the actual live audio meter, and confirm the transcript appears. Review capture permissions and try Listen again if the meter is flat. The Mac transcription guide explains the steps.

Trial and monthly activation

The device-bound trial lasts 10 minutes from your first session. Monthly software access costs USD 20, with an approximate INR reference conversion for India. Open Activate in the app to prepare an email containing your Device ID. Payment instructions, activation, and renewal are arranged manually with Udhay; there is no automatic card charge.

Already have a key?

Paste the issued key into the app’s activation field. Renewals can extend the same key. For a device change, activation error, or expired access, email udhaybhat00@gmail.com with your app version, platform, and Device ID. Keep the actual license key private.

Report a reproducible issue

Include your app version, operating system, chip architecture, chosen speech and answer models, and the steps that trigger the problem. Redact private conversation text, API keys, license keys, and Device IDs from public screenshots or issues. Contact udhaybhat00@gmail.com for purchase questions; use the public GitHub issue tracker for non-sensitive bug reports.
